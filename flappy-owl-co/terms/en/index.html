3. About the game

Flappy Owl & Co is a one-touch flying arcade game developed with Godot 4. You can choose an owl or other birds and flying animals, pass obstacles with increasing difficulty, earn points and try to beat your personal best. Sound, character and language settings are available in Spanish, English and Brazilian Portuguese.

The version described offers single-player sessions on your device. It has no multiplayer, chat, social features or online leaderboards. Scores have no monetary value and do not entitle you to prizes or payments.
