1. Scope

This policy explains how information is handled in Flappy Owl & Co for Android (package com.byka.vuelonocturno), version 1.0.0, and distinguishes playing the game, email inquiries and visits to these legal pages.

The game works offline, without registration or sign-in. Its features do not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. It includes no ads, in-app purchases, analytics, tracking or third-party crash reporting services.

2. Responsible developer

Bryan Inostroza develops and publishes Flappy Owl & Co under the name BYKA Tech, from Chile. Privacy and support contact: bryan.inostroza.dev@gmail.com.

3. Information handled

The device stores your personal best score, sound preference, selected character and chosen language (including the automatic option). They are kept in the application’s private internal storage. The system language is read locally when the automatic option is selected; it is not sent to the developer.

The score and state of a run are processed in memory while you play. The current run is not saved for resuming after closing the game, and no history of runs is transmitted.

If you contact the developer by email, the developer receives your email address, message content and any information or files you choose to attach. You do not need to send sensitive data or your best score to request help.

4. Purposes and use

Your best score is used to display your personal record; settings remember your sound, character and language choices. This information stays on your device and is not used to identify you, build profiles, advertise or produce developer statistics.

Information you send by email is used to answer your inquiry, provide support and follow up on your request or complaint. It is not used for advertising.

5. Permissions and connections

The application does not request Internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or shared storage permissions. Saving settings in its private space does not require access to your shared files.

Playing does not establish server connections. If you choose to open the Terms and Conditions or web privacy policy from the game, the link opens in your browser and requires a connection. The link does not include your score, settings or player identifiers; the language path only selects the translation.

6. Third parties and website visits

The game uses the Godot engine and components whose licenses are available under “Privacy and licenses”. It integrates no advertising, analytics, account, payment or data collection SDKs. BYKA Tech does not receive, sell or share the best score or settings stored on your device.

Google Play and Android may process information for their own services, such as distribution, updates and diagnostics depending on your settings. This processing is independent of the game and governed by Google’s privacy policy.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub logs and stores your IP address for security purposes. Your browser and connection provider also participate in the visit. These pages add no cookies, analytics, tracking pixels or forms, and fonts are served from the same website.

If you write to the Gmail contact, email providers process the message to deliver and store it. Google and GitHub may process information outside your country under their policies. The game does not transfer its local data to those providers.

7. Storage and security

Your best score and settings are saved in a configuration file in the application’s private space, protected by Android access controls. The game does not add its own encryption to that file. Automatic Android backup is disabled in this version; there is no synchronization or progress backup on developer servers.

Support email is stored in the email service used to handle it, separately from the game’s local data. The web policy is served over HTTPS. No system offers an absolute security guarantee.

8. Retention

Your best score and settings stay on the device until you clear the application’s data or uninstall it; changing preferences replaces their previous values. The game does not apply an expiration period to these data. The in-memory run state is not retained after closing the game.

Retention of support messages depends on handling and following up on the inquiry and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion using the contact provided. This policy sets no automatic deletion period for email or for records managed by Google or GitHub under their own policies.

9. Deletion and your rights

To delete your best score and settings, open Android Settings > Apps > Flappy Owl & Co > Storage (or Storage and cache) > Clear data or Clear storage. You can also uninstall the game. Clearing only the cache does not delete these data. Menu names may vary by device.

Deletion of your best score and settings is permanent: BYKA Tech has no copy to restore them and no remote access to delete them. There is no game account to delete.

To ask about information sent by email or exercise rights of access, correction, deletion or other rights available under applicable law, write to bryan.inostroza.dev@gmail.com. Describe your request and the related email address; do not send identity documents unless needed and requested with justification. Requests will be handled according to law, including mandatory retention exceptions. You can contact the competent authority.

10. Children

The game does not ask for age or collect children’s personal data through its features. The same local practices without tracking apply to all players. If you believe a child has sent personal information to support by email, contact the developer to request review and deletion where appropriate.

11. Policy changes

This policy may be updated to reflect game changes or legal requirements. The current version will be published at this URL with its update date. If a future version changes how data is handled, notice will be provided and any required consent obtained before that processing begins. The three translations are intended to describe the same practices.

For privacy or support inquiries: Bryan Inostroza — BYKA Tech, Chile, bryan.inostroza.dev@gmail.com. See also the Terms and Conditions for Flappy Owl & Co.
